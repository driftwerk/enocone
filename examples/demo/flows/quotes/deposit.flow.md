# Client pays a 10% deposit to start work, and the rest at completion

Firms want to start work sooner. Taking a deposit when the quote is accepted
lets them order searches straight away, with the balance due at completion.

## Rules

- The deposit is 10% of the quote total, rounded up to the nearest pound.
- Searches are paid in full up front, because the firm pays for them immediately.

## Given

1. **A quote of £1,368.00 exists, including £228 of searches**

## When

2. **Jane accepts the quote and is asked to pay the deposit**

3. **Jane pays the deposit by card**

## Then

4. **Jane is told how much is left to pay at completion**

5. **The case handler sees “Deposit paid” on the lead**

## Open questions

- Is VAT on the legal fee part of the 10%, or charged at completion?
- Can a case handler waive the deposit for repeat clients?
