# Client accepts a quote from the email

Used by enocone's own suite: a draft that reviewers comment on.

## Rules

- The link in the quote email expires after 14 days.
- Accepting twice shows the quote as already accepted.

## Given

1. **A quote has been sent to Jane**

## When

2. **Jane opens the link in the email and accepts**

## Then

3. **The quote shows as accepted**
